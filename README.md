# Guild Control 735 for UWoW Legion

**Your guild is more than a list of names.**

Who is ready for keystones? Who has the specialization your group needs? Which raids have your guildmates cleared, and how active are they in PvP? Guild Control brings the roster, character profiles and statistics together so those answers are easier to find.

The addon is built specifically for **UWoW Legion 7.3.5**. It is useful to anyone who wants to get to know their guild: find people to play with, explore its activity or put a group together. Guild masters and authorized members also have access to management tools.

## A roster you can work with

Resize the window to suit your screen, keep the columns you need and adjust their widths. Search for a guildmate by name or narrow the roster with filters. Select a player to move from a roster entry to their character card and detailed statistics.

## Character information without relying on memory

Character profiles keep equipment, specializations, professions, and main/alt relationships close at hand. Members maintain their own information, giving the guild a shared source of character details without squeezing everything into short guild notes.

## See your guild's activity

The Mythic+, raid and PvP sections help you explore guild activity, view individual results, assess progress and find players with similar interests.

**Russian and English interface. Customizable tables. Shared information for playing together.**

## Guild data

![Guild data](docs/images/general-data.png)

**From finding players to managing the roster, all in one spacious window.**

The Guild data tab turns the guild list into a practical working table. Browse members, find characters for your group, invite newcomers and manage the roster. The main tools are close together: filter the list, select players and choose an action.

### More room for the information you need

Resize the window to suit your screen, adjust column widths and hide information you are not using. Give names and notes more room, or keep a compact set of details for a particular task.

Both vertical and horizontal scrolling are available. **Reset widths** restores the default column widths, while **Columns** controls which columns are visible.

### Find players without scanning the whole roster

Use text search and combine filters to find members by:

- class and guild rank;
- level range;
- online status and time since last login;
- professions;
- availability of addon data and specializations.

Find online characters of a particular class, guildmates with a profession you need, or members who have been away for a while. The counter below the table shows how many characters match your selection. **Reset filters** returns you to the full list.

### Character details close at hand

The table includes name, class, level, guild rank, specializations, main/alt relationships, last online, zone, notes and professions. Officer information is available when your guild permissions allow it.

Select a member to open their **Player card** on the right, with access to available Mythic+, raid and PvP data. Start with the roster and move straight to details about a particular player.

### Main and alternate characters

Guild Control helps you recognize the players behind the character names. Main/alt relationships appear in the roster, making it easier to understand whose character you are looking at.

The special **`[GC:MAIN]`** marker in a guild note identifies a main character. This provides an explicit main designation alongside the other roster information.

The user guide explains where to place the marker, which permissions are required and how alternate characters are linked.

### Invitations and roster management

Invite newcomers with **Invite to guild**, and work with existing members through **Manage guild roster**.

With the necessary guild permissions, you can remove members or change their ranks, including through **bulk actions**. Prepare a selection for a roster cleanup or a rank change across several characters without repeating the same operation for each one.

Search and filters help you prepare the list before selecting members. Available actions depend on your character's guild rank and permissions.

### Refreshing data and sending reminders

**Refresh roster** requests the current guild roster. **Request data** starts collecting Guild Control information within your permissions. Use **Stop request** to cancel the current request.

The profile reminder button lets you remind guild members in chat to update their character information. Reminder options are configured separately.

### Guild information beside the roster

The **Information** and **Message of the day** side tabs open the corresponding guild texts. Read, select and copy their contents without leaving the addon window.

### Alongside the standard interface

Guild Control brings together tools for everyday roster work and member information. Some Blizzard sections and functions deliberately remain in the standard guild window, which stays available and complements the addon.

## My card

![My card](docs/images/my-card.png)

**Your equipment profiles and characters, without asking an officer to update your guild note.**

Changed your gear or put together a set for another specialization? Save your equipped set in My card while the guild master or a member of a trusted rank is online. This lets you update your information yourself without asking an officer.

### Show the specializations you play

Your card presents your equipment profiles: specializations and their corresponding equipment levels, or gear scores. Guildmates can see the role and gear you can bring to a group.

After changing your gear, save the set again. Previously saved equipment profiles do not update automatically.

### Share your raid experience

The card also shares your character's raid achievement information, helping guildmates learn about your raid experience.

Mythic+ and PvP activity belong to separate analytics sections and are not transmitted through the card.

### Link your characters

Identify your main and alternate characters in the same guild. This helps officers see which characters belong to one player and take those relationships into account when managing the roster.

Instead of unrelated names, the guild gets a clearer picture of your characters and available equipment profiles.

## Analytics

![Analytics](docs/images/analytics.png)

**See what your guild is playing.**

Keystones, raids or PvP: every guild has its own interests. Analytics helps you explore member activity, view results and find people to play with.

It serves the whole guild: members can find like-minded players, while leadership can assess activity and plan guild events.

### Mythic+

View available information about Mythic Keystone dungeon runs. Compare member results and find players to continue progressing through dungeons with.

This gives you a picture of who participates in Mythic+ and the results they achieve.

### Raids

Explore members' raid progress: raids, difficulties and defeated bosses. The overview helps you see who has experience with the content you need and how raid achievements are distributed across the roster.

This information is useful when preparing a raid or getting to know a new guild.

### PvP

View available member statistics across PvP modes. Select a mode and period to compare results and find players with similar interests.

### From the overview to an individual player

Use the guild overview to find members of interest, then explore a selected character's results. Combine analytics with the roster and character cards to consider experience, specializations and equipment together.

## Settings

![Settings](docs/images/settings.png)

**Trusted ranks and help with errors.**

This tab is primarily for guild leadership. You usually do not need it for everyday play: it controls who may request data and contains tools for reporting a problem.

### For guild leadership

The guild master selects **Trusted ranks**, whose members may request Guild Control data. **Save and share with guild** saves and distributes that selection.

Several trusted members can help maintain data exchange, so the guild master does not have to be online at all times.

### If something goes wrong

The **Error recording** section lets you start diagnostic recording, repeat the problematic action and stop recording. The resulting materials can help identify the cause.

**Report a bug** displays the GitHub Issues address for submitting a description and reports. The user guide provides the detailed procedure.

### Version and updates

The installed addon version is shown here. **Download new version** lets you copy the GitHub Releases address.

## Network protection

![Network protection](docs/images/network-protection.png)

**Automatic exchange protection and manual control for the guild master.**

Guild Control limits exchanges with clients that violate data-transfer rules or generate excessive requests. Automatic protection runs for each member and needs no everyday configuration.

### Managing restrictions

The guild master can temporarily block individual characters from exchanging addon data or remove an existing manual ban. Trusted ranks do not grant permission to manage these restrictions.

Search, filters and group actions help select members. The table shows restriction reasons and remaining durations. Automatic restrictions expire on their own, while saved manual bans remain active when the guild master is offline.

A ban affects Guild Control data exchange only. It does not remove the character from the guild or change their guild permissions.
